## What to extract

Build a structured record, one row per **collision** — two or more tasks targeting the same file, symbol, or hunk:

- Colliding task identities (or explicitly `unknown`).
- The contested path and symbol.
- Who won, and the stated reason.
- **Resolution basis** — classify: staleness of one patch, presence/absence of a test, a safety boundary, a measured RED result, compatibility (both kept), or unresolved.
- **Was ownership attributable at all?** This is the headline variable. Count paths where the adjudicator wrote `unknown` because no agent had filed a report naming itself.
- **Was any change observed to appear and then disappear** — staged then reverted to committed content — and could the adjudicator distinguish a deliberate revert from another writer's overwrite?
- Whether the losing work was destroyed or preserved.

## The three questions this is for

1. **Attributability rate.** Of all contested paths, what fraction had a known owning task? Ownership here is reconstructed from self-filed reports — testimony, not instrumentation. Quantify how often testimony was absent.
2. **Unattributable reversals.** How many times did work vanish with no way to tell revert from clobber? Even one is meaningful; report the exact count and quote each in full.
3. **Isolation staleness vs harvest quality.** `cloud-mirror.js` records that a branch 5 commits behind harvested clean while one 467 behind produced empty applies, fixes already present, and citations to unrelated code. Find every other instance where harvest quality is tied to base staleness. Extract any numbers: commits behind, applied vs skipped hunk counts, RED/GREEN outcomes. **Is there enough to plot harvest quality against staleness, even crudely?** If there are only two points, say exactly that — two points is not a curve.

## Discipline

- **Quote, do not paraphrase.** Every count must be traceable to quoted text with a file and line.
- These reports are **agent-written testimony about agents' own work.** They are not instrumentation and may be self-serving, incomplete, or wrong. State this as a limitation of every claim derived from them. Where a report's claim about the code is checkable against the code, check it and report agreement or disagreement.
- Some reports reference files that no longer exist or were renamed. Record the dangling reference rather than resolving it silently.
- Do not infer a collision that no report asserts. Absence of a report is not absence of a collision — say so.
